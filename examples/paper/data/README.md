# Reference data

`ico_s3o2_exact_heat_capacity.csv` — exact heat capacity C(T) (columns: T in units of
J/k_B, C in units of k_B; 5000 points, T = 1e-5 ... 5) of the antiferromagnetic s = 3/2
Heisenberg icosahedron (J = 1), obtained by complete diagonalization with simultaneous
spin and point-group adaptation in

> S. Ghassemi Tabrizi, T. D. Kühne, Simultaneous spin and point-group adaptation in exact
> diagonalization of spin clusters, Magnetism 5 (2025) 8.

Used by `study_exact_icosahedron.m`.
